When you start a sync, VocaNum may synchronize supported learning records through the private CloudKit database associated with your iCloud account. Local learning continues when iCloud is unavailable, restricted, interrupted, or not used. VocaNum does not require you to purchase Pro in order to access this private iCloud synchronization flow.

If you delete local learning data and want that deletion reflected across devices, allow a successful synchronization after the deletion before relying on another device’s stored copy.
