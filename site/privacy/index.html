Privacy Policy

VocaNum works without an account. Local learning is available to everyone; Pro unlocks six learning protocols and three vocabulary packs. Optional private iCloud sync is available separately.

Last updated: August 9, 2026

In short: VocaNum does not operate an account system, show ads, track you across apps, or sell or share your information. Local learning remains available offline. VocaNum sends limited aggregate product-analytics events when the analytics service is configured; optional private iCloud sync is separate and sends selected learning records to your private Apple database.

1. Who is responsible for VocaNum?

VocaNum is provided by Kerem ÖZTÜRK, referred to as “VocaNum,” “we,” or “us” in this policy. Contact information is provided below.

2. Information stored by the app

VocaNum stores onboarding choices, practice preferences, question history, vocabulary progress, confidence, review timing, scores, and practice-session summaries in local app storage. This information operates the app, personalizes practice, calculates progress, and displays history. Normal vocabulary and question content is bundled with the app.

3. Pro Precision and optional private iCloud sync

Pro unlocks six Pro Precision learning protocols and three focused vocabulary packs with 150 questions each. Base learning, offline practice, progress, export, deletion, and optional private iCloud sync remain available without Pro.

If you turn on Profile → Backup & Sync, VocaNum sends profile and preferences, vocabulary states, progress, answer records, scores, and session summaries to the private database belonging to your iCloud account. The app does not send these synced learning records to a VocaNum-operated server. They are stored in the private CloudKit database and are not readable by VocaNum.

4. First-party product analytics

When the production analytics service is configured, VocaNum queues limited events locally and uploads them later when connectivity is available. Analytics may include a pseudonymous installation/session identifier, app version/build, operating-system version, device model, locale, event names, and aggregate interaction, timing, and purchase-funnel properties.

Analytics does not receive question text, definitions, word identifiers, selected answers, typed input, profile fields, receipts, transaction IDs, or payment details. It is used for product analytics and operational anomaly signals, not advertising, cross-app tracking, entitlement decisions, or authoritative scores. Offline practice does not depend on analytics.

5. Apple services and payments

Apple processes purchase and subscription transactions through StoreKit. VocaNum does not receive payment-card or bank information. Apple may process information related to downloading or using apps under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

6. Sharing, selling, and advertising

We do not sell personal information, share learning activity for behavioral advertising, or disclose it to advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDK.

7. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. Open Profile → Learning Data to reset progress, reset question history, export data, or delete local learning data. If private sync is enabled, a deletion marker is propagated on the next successful sync. Apple may retain service-level data or deletion metadata under Apple’s terms and policies.

8. Children’s privacy

VocaNum does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The app does not provide accounts, messaging, public profiles, or user-generated content.

9. This website

These pages do not use advertising cookies or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may temporarily process technical request information to deliver and secure the site under its own terms. The app’s first-party analytics is a separate app service.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to keremoztrkdev@gmail.com. You can also visit Support or read the Terms of Use.
