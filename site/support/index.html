Frequently asked questions

Does VocaNum require internet access?

No. Normal vocabulary and learning features work offline after installation.

Do I need an account?

No VocaNum account or sign-in is required. Pro private sync uses the iCloud account already configured on your Apple device, if you choose to enable it.

How does Pro private iCloud sync work?

Purchase Pro, open Profile → Backup & Sync, and enable the toggle after signing in to iCloud. VocaNum stores profile, preferences, progress, answers, scores, and sessions in your private CloudKit database so your Apple devices can share them. The app stays usable locally if sync is unavailable.

How do I restore or manage a Pro purchase?

Open Profile → Membership. Use Restore Purchases to ask Apple to refresh purchases, or useManage Subscriptions to open Apple’s subscription management screen. Monthly and annual Pro plans renew through Apple; Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase.

How do I reset or delete my data?

In the app, open Profile → Learning Data. You can export, reset progress, reset question history, or delete all local learning data. If sync is enabled, keep it enabled until a successful sync records the deletion for your other devices.

What happens if two devices change the same record?

VocaNum merges records using a deterministic last-change rule. A newer timestamp wins; deletion wins on an exact timestamp tie, and stable device and payload tie-breakers make the result consistent. The app does not silently discard local data when iCloud is unavailable.

Why did a word’s status or score change?

Correct, incorrect, and “don’t know” responses can affect confidence, review timing, and whether a word is known, forgotten, or unmeasured.

Which devices are supported?

The current app build requires iOS or iPadOS 18.1 or later.

Troubleshooting

  1. Close and reopen VocaNum.
  2. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
  3. Install the latest compatible VocaNum and system updates.
  4. Use a reset option only after reviewing its confirmation. Reset actions cannot be undone through VocaNum support.

If the issue continues, contact support before deleting the app if you want to preserve its current local data.

Report a problem

Please include:

  • VocaNum version and build number.
  • Device model and iOS or iPadOS version.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • Steps that reproduce the problem.
  • A screenshot, if useful and safe to share.

Send the report tokeremoztrkdev@gmail.com.
