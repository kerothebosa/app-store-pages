Frequently asked questions

Does VocaNum require internet access?

No. Normal vocabulary and learning features work offline after installation.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro unlocks six Pro Precision learning protocols—Daily Precision Mission, Error Atlas, Confidence Lab, Recovery Casefiles, Guided Trails, and Weekly Learning Brief—and three focused vocabulary packs with 150 questions each. Base learning and offline practice remain available without Pro.

How do I restore or manage a Pro purchase?

Open Profile → Membership. Use Restore Purchases to ask Apple to refresh purchases, or use Manage Subscriptions to open Apple’s subscription management screen. Monthly and annual Pro plans renew through Apple; Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase.

How does optional private iCloud sync work?

Open Profile → Backup & Sync and enable the toggle after signing in to iCloud. This optional free feature stores profile, preferences, progress, answers, scores, and sessions in your private CloudKit database. The app stays usable locally if sync is unavailable.

Does analytics prevent offline play?

No. Practice works offline. When configured, VocaNum queues limited aggregate product-analytics events locally and uploads them later; it does not send question text, words, selected answers, typed input, receipts, or transaction IDs.

How do I reset or delete my data?

In the app, open Profile → Learning Data. You can export, reset progress, reset question history, or delete all local learning data.

Which devices are supported?

The current app build requires iOS or iPadOS 18.1 or later.

Troubleshooting

  1. Close and reopen VocaNum.
  2. Restart your iPhone or iPad.
  3. Install the latest compatible VocaNum and system updates.
  4. Use a reset option only after reviewing its confirmation. Reset actions cannot be undone through VocaNum support.

If the issue continues, contact support before deleting the app if you want to preserve its current local data.
