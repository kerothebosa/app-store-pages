Privacy Policy

VocaNum works without an account. Local learning is available to everyone; Pro can optionally synchronize learning data to your private iCloud database.

Last updated: August 3, 2026

In short: VocaNum does not operate an account system, show ads, track you, or sell or share your information. Learning progress and settings are stored locally unless you choose Pro private iCloud sync. When enabled, selected app data is sent to your private Apple iCloud database; VocaNum cannot access that data.

1. Who is responsible for VocaNum?

VocaNum is provided by Kerem ÖZTÜRK, referred to as “VocaNum,” “we,” or “us” in this policy. Contact information is provided in Section 10.

2. Information stored by the app

To provide its learning and progress features, VocaNum stores the following information inside the app’s local storage on your device:

  • Onboarding choices, starting difficulty, daily goal, and practice preferences.
  • Question history, including the question, selected answer or “don’t know” choice, correctness, response time, and answer date.
  • Vocabulary progress, confidence, known or forgotten status, review timing, scores, and practice-session summaries.

This information is used only on your device to operate the app, personalize practice, calculate progress, and display your history. Normal vocabulary and question content is bundled with the app.

3. Optional Pro private iCloud sync

VocaNum Pro includes optional backup and synchronization through Apple CloudKit. If you purchase Pro and turn on Profile → Backup & Sync, VocaNum sends the following app data to the private database belonging to your iCloud account:

  • Your VocaNum profile and learning preferences.
  • Vocabulary states, confidence, review timing, and progress.
  • Answer records, score events, and session summaries.

The app does not send this data to a VocaNum-operated server. The records are stored in the app’s private CloudKit database and are not readable by VocaNum. Sync requires an available iCloud account and can be disabled at any time. If iCloud is unavailable, the app continues to work with local data.

4. Information we collect

VocaNum does not receive the learning records described above. The app has no VocaNum account, analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, social features, or developer-operated cloud storage. Apple processes purchase and subscription transactions through StoreKit; VocaNum does not receive your payment details.

The app can verify whether Apple has granted a Pro entitlement, but we cannot view, retrieve, identify, or restore the contents of your private CloudKit records.

5. Device backups and Apple services

Depending on your device and backup settings, the operating system may include app data in an iCloud or computer backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by VocaNum. Apple may process information related to downloading or using apps under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy.

6. Sharing, selling, and advertising

We do not sell personal information, share personal information for behavioral advertising, or disclose your learning activity to advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

7. Retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it. In VocaNum, open Profile → Learning Data to reset progress, reset question history, export data, or delete all local learning data.

When private sync is enabled, a local deletion is recorded as a deletion marker and propagated on the next successful sync. This prevents another device from restoring the deleted record. Keep sync enabled until that sync completes; disabling sync first stops future propagation from that device. Apple may retain service-level data or deletion metadata under Apple’s terms and policies.

You can also delete the app. A device backup or private iCloud record may retain an earlier copy until it is removed through the relevant Apple controls or by synchronized deletion.

8. Children’s privacy

VocaNum does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The app does not provide accounts, messaging, public profiles, or user-generated content.

9. Security

VocaNum limits privacy risk by keeping learning records on your device or in your private CloudKit database. Your device protections, iCloud account security, and backup settings help protect data, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. This website

These pages do not use advertising cookies or analytics scripts. The hosting provider may temporarily process technical request information, such as IP address, browser details, requested page, and timestamp, to deliver and secure the site under its own terms.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent tokeremoztrkdev@gmail.com. You can also visit the VocaNum support page or read the Terms of Use.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if VocaNum’s features or legal obligations change. The revised policy will be posted here with a new “Last updated” date. If a future version begins collecting data, this policy and the App Store disclosures will be updated before collection begins.
